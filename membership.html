Membership

Membership entitles each member to represent the Association within the assigned market in which the member resides and conducts business.

Market coverage

Limitation of the number of members per country

The network keeps membership deliberately scarce, so that every member holds real value in their market.

Standard markets

A maximum of 4 members per country.

Smaller markets

The Board will determine, in certain small markets, where 1 to 2 members will suffice.

China, USA and India

An exception applies to China, the USA and India, where up to 6 members are admitted.

Annual dues

Dues are set by market share

Annual membership dues are based on the IMF / WTO market share of the member country as a guideline. Dues for each member category are set by the Board of Directors.

Membership is on a yearly basis, renewable every 12 months.

Tier 1 Market

Full membership due

Applied to the network’s largest markets by share.

Tier 2 Market

Two thirds of full membership due

Applied to mid-share markets across the network.

Tier 3 Market

One third of full membership due

Applied to smaller markets within the network.

Terms

Conditions of membership

  • Membership dues are payable within 30 days after receipt of invoice. Banking fees are on the account of the members.
  • Acceptance of membership is finalised after membership dues are received.
  • Payment of membership dues implies acceptance of the network rules and ethics.
  • All members accept and understand that any and all transactions between members are subject to their own risk. Partners Unlimited is not in any way held accountable or responsible.
  • All members are advised to inform the Board when other members are delinquent in payments — recommended 60 days from date of invoice, unless otherwise agreed between the members.
