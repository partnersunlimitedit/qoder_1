Meetings

Meetings are where the relationships that make the network work are built and maintained.

AGM

Annual General Meeting

The purpose of the AGM is to determine major issues, and all senior management members are invited to attend.

The meeting also aims at enrichment of the relationship among senior managers of the network members. Bilateral meetings will be presented among members.

Attendance requirement

Members are encouraged to attend every meeting. Attendance is mandatory once every 2 years. Failure to attend consecutive meetings without good cause may be ground for termination of membership.

Meeting packages

Meeting packages are all-inclusive, except travelling cost.

GSM

Global Sales Meeting (as needed)

The purpose of the GSM is to give members’ sales professionals a platform to meet and develop sales strategy bilaterally.

Meetings will be part instructional, including actual success cases, and part bilateral one-to-one meetings.

Send your team. All members are encouraged to send their sales development team to the meeting.
