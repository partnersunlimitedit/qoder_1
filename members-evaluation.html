Members Evaluation

All members are objectively evaluated by members of their peers, so that standards across the network stay high.

Programme

Annual Members Evaluation Program (EP)

All members will be objectively evaluated by members of their peers.

Members will be evaluated in the categories listed here, subject to revision. Each category carries a 10 point value.

Read how the network is governed

Scored 0 – 10 points by peer members.

Performance standard

How the scoring works

5 categoriesCommunication, sales activity, accounting, operation efficiency and others.
10 pointsEach category carries a 10 point value.
50% minimumMembers must maintain a minimum score of 50% efficiency.
Three year review. Members that do not maintain the minimum score for 3 consecutive years may be asked to leave the network.

Ethics

Ethics and code of conduct

All members pledge to:

  • Share business within the network, with the ultimate goal of 60%.
  • Meet members’ responsibility — protecting the network and the interest of all members.
  • Share resources.
  • Build trade lanes mutually.
  • Treat each other with respect.

Financial protection

Debt protection

Partners Unlimited will build up a Debt Protection Fund to cover members for non-disputed, unpaid invoices of a Partners Unlimited member.

Compulsory cover

The Debt Protection Program is compulsory for all members.

Built by members

Funds are built up by the members on a yearly contribution basis.

Defined in the appendix

Details or application of the rules are defined in an appendix.
