How We Operate

For better management of growth, the network sub-divides the global market into four regions based on geographical region.

Structure

Four regions, one network

Regionalisation allows for localised expertise while maintaining a global reach.

Region 1The Americas
Region 2The Asia – Pacific
Region 3The Africa – Mideast
Region 4The EU

Governance

Represented by the members themselves

Qualified members are invited to the Advisory Board to represent the views and interests of the members.

These members will form the Management Board of Directors, holding position as Regional Director for the four regions.

A

Advisory Board

Represents the views and interests of the membership across the network.

B

Management Board of Directors

Formed from qualified members, holding position as Regional Director for the four regions.

Eligibility

Becoming a member

  • Active members of the Association must be private enterprises or corporations, involved in the freight forwarding industry and actively supporting the purpose and objectives of the Association.
  • Applicants that meet the membership standards may apply in writing or online to the Chairman of the Board for membership.
  • Subject to approval by the Board of Directors, the applicant may be accepted as a member of the network. The Board may reject an application without comments.
  • Membership of the Association entitles each member to represent the Association within the assigned market in which the member resides and conducts business.
  • Membership of the Association may only promote the offices where they are assigned within the network.

Resignation

Leaving the network

Any member may resign by giving three months’ notice to the Board of Directors, by end of month. The annual membership fee remains due and no refund is granted.

Note. The annual membership fee remains due on resignation, and no refund is granted.

Disqualification

Members may be disqualified on the following grounds

01

Conduct against the network

Conduct that would disgrace or be against the network.

02

Sustained under-performance

Consecutive performance below expectations for 3 years.

03

Criminal offence

A criminal conviction attributable to the member.

04

Unpaid membership dues

Failure to settle the annual membership dues.

05

Unpaid member settlements

Failure to settle payments to other members.
